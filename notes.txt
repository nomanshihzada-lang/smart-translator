What's New in Version 1.1:

1. Universal Screen Text Translation: Now you can translate any focused message or text anywhere on your screen (WhatsApp chats, notifications, browsers, and apps) even outside edit boxes.
2. Full Message & Multi-Line Extraction: Automatically collects complete text across nested screen containers so no part of a message is missed.
3. Smart Focus Priority: Prevents empty input boxes from blocking translation while reading messages in chat apps.
4. Unified Single Language Selector: Simplified to one clear language selector with accurate Checked / Not Checked status across all 249 languages.
